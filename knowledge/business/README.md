# knowledge/business

What the business is and what it sells. Identity, offer, pricing authority, price calibration,
platform safety, and the money register.

Client-specific state does not live here. It lives in `knowledge/clients/<client>/`.

## Who owns what

Every row is the single owner of that fact. Anything else that mentions it points here.

| Fact | Owner |
|---|---|
| Positioning, ICP, the problem, proof, what we refuse to do | `brand_foundation.md` |
| The productized offer, its price, its raise rule, its gate | `outbound-offer.md` |
| Quote -> reaction -> outcome record, for price calibration | `pricing-outcomes.md` |
| Structured profile that AI drafting reads | `operator-business-profile.json` |
| LinkedIn volume envelope and the stop signals | `linkedin-safety-rules.md` |
| Invoice numbering, per-invoice records, the cash register | `invoices/` |
| Collected, outstanding, burn, concentration | `MONEY.md`, generated by `scripts/money` |

Deliberately NOT here:

- **Engagement prices** -> the client's own `knowledge/clients/<client>/roadmap.md`.
- **Fixed monthly costs** -> `knowledge/ops/tools-and-stack.md`.
- **Wire and account details** -> the gitignored `PAYMENT-DETAILS.md` under `invoices/`. The
  committed `invoices/PAYMENT-DETAILS.template.md` is the placeholder scaffold.
- **Rulings** -> `ai/DECISIONS.md`. A ruling restated here is a second copy with no review date.
- **Retired numbers** -> `ai/TOMBSTONES.md`. Never re-typed here, even as an aside.

## Rules that bite in this directory

1. **One owner per number.** `outbound-offer.md` states the productized price. No other file in
   the repo states it, including this README. If you find a second copy, delete it and leave a
   pointer, in the same session.
2. **`MONEY.md` is generated.** Do not hand-edit it. Change `invoices/SUMMARY.md`, then run
   `scripts/money`. `repo-doctor` flags a rollup older than its source.
3. **`pricing-outcomes.md` is append-mostly.** `skills/proposal-creator` opens a row on send,
   `skills/invoice-creator` closes it on collection. Both are sanctioned writes. Do not rewrite
   a closed row to make the record look better than it was.
4. **`operator-business-profile.json` is merged, never rebuilt.** `skills/business-profile-creator`
   diffs it field by field. A blank-slate regenerate silently drops every field the interview
   does not ask about.
5. **Perishable pages carry an expiry line.** `linkedin-safety-rules.md` is the one in here, and
   it declares a `Stale after` date. When it expires, re-verify the numbers against the platform,
   do not just move the date.

## Using this as a template

`brand_foundation.md`, `outbound-offer.md` and `operator-business-profile.json` are filled with
the worked example (Northwind Labs). Rewrite them for your own operation. Keep the structure and
the ownership table above, which is the part that stops a price from rotting in four files.

`pricing-outcomes.md` should be emptied to its header row on first use. Your calibration record
is worth more than someone else's.
