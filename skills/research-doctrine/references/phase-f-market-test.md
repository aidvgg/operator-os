# Phase F: Reality Test

Use Phase F when the recommendation contains a bet that research cannot settle. Design the smallest real-world test that can invalidate the bet. Do not substitute additional desk research for the test result.

Choose the fitting branch:

- **Market test:** demand, willingness to pay, behavior, adoption, channel, or positioning
- **Operational test:** feasibility, reliability, integration, workflow, or capacity
- **Verification test:** professional consultation, official inquiry, filing, inspection, or authoritative determination
