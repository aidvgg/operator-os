# Pain point bank

The readable bank: what the people {{BUSINESS}} sells to say is broken, who feels it, what it
costs them, and what {{BUSINESS}} would offer against it.

`pain-point-bank.csv` in this folder is the machine-readable mirror of this file. Same entries,
same ids, same values. Edit both in the same pass or the mirror is a lie. Schema, the quality
bar, and the promotion rule live in `knowledge/pain-points/README.md`.

**No prices in this file.** Offer angles describe what gets delivered, never what it costs to buy.
Sell-side pricing has exactly one owner per motion: `knowledge/business/outbound-offer.md` for
the standard offer, the client's roadmap file in `knowledge/clients/` for an engagement. Dollar
figures in an entry are the *prospect's* cost of the problem, always marked as an estimate.
